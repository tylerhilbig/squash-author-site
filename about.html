Hi, my name is Josh

I write science fiction and fantasy stories that focus on complex characters and innovative settings. I'm glad you stopped by. I've written a lot over the years. It can be a little daunting to get into my work, but it doesn't have to be. Most of my series can be read independently of one another, and although many of them are connected behind the scenes, references to these things are generally easter eggs or other small-scale connections.
