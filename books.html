Discover the World of Squash

Stormlight Archive

On a world battered by endless storms and ancient wars, unlikely heroes begin to uncover long-buried secrets that could change the fate of civilization itself. The Stormlight Archive blends epic battles, deep political intrigue, mysterious magic, and unforgettable character journeys into a sweeping fantasy saga about honor, sacrifice, and the power of hope in the face of impossible odds.

Twilight

When a quiet teenager moves to a small, rainy town in the Pacific Northwest, she finds herself drawn into a mysterious world hidden just beneath the surface of ordinary life. Twilight is a romantic fantasy series filled with suspense, supernatural secrets, and intense emotional connections that explore love, danger, and the challenges of belonging.

Percy Jackson & the Olympians

What if the gods of ancient mythology were still alive today—and their children were walking among us? Percy Jackson and the Olympians follows a modern teenager who discovers he has a powerful connection to the world of Greek gods and monsters. Packed with humor, fast-paced adventure, and mythological twists, the series delivers an exciting journey of friendship, courage, and self-discovery.
